What you get

An objective number you can stand behind.

A visual inspection can't tell you how slippery a floor really is. A pendulum test can — and it gives you the result in writing.

The pendulum test is the HSE's preferred method for measuring slip resistance, because it works in the wet conditions where slips actually happen. We test to the current British and European standard and the UK Slip Resistance Group's guidelines, so your result means the same thing to an insurer, a client or a court.

  • Wet and dry. Most slips happen on a wet or contaminated floor, so the wet result is usually the one that matters.
  • Three directions. The floor is tested whichever way people walk across it.
  • To BS EN 16165:2021 (Annex C) and the UKSRG Guidelines, Issue 6 (Jan 2024).
  • The right slider for the area — one for areas where people wear shoes, another for barefoot areas such as pool surrounds and changing rooms.
  • On-site, or from samples sent to our lab.
  • A digital report, emailed to you, for compliance and due diligence.

Questions

Booking a pendulum test: your questions.

How long does a test take?

A typical on-site test takes a couple of hours, depending on the number of areas.

What do I get afterwards?

A digital report, emailed to you, with the Pendulum Test Value for each area — the evidence you need for compliance and due diligence.

Is the pendulum test recognised by the HSE?

Yes. The HSE describes the pendulum as its preferred test method, because it is portable and works in the wet conditions where slips occur.

Can you test a sample instead of visiting?

Yes. As well as testing on-site, we can test samples sent to our lab.

What if my floor fails?

A low result doesn't mean the floor has to come out. An anti-slip treatment can often raise the slip resistance of your existing floor. After treatment we target a PTV of 36+, re-test and issue a Certificate of Conformity as your paper trail.

Do you cover my area?

Yes. Rodal works nationwide and has done since 2003. We're based in Banwell, North Somerset.
