Baths, shower trays & wet areas

Why treatment beats replacement.

Enamel baths and acrylic shower trays are among the most common slip risks in a hotel bedroom — and the most disruptive to put right by ripping them out.

Replacing a bath or a shower tray means a room out of action, a plumber, a tiler and a bill. An anti-slip treatment works on the surface itself, so the fitting stays exactly where it is — and the room goes back into service instead of into a refurbishment schedule.

  • The room keeps earning. No strip-out, no re-tiling, no waiting on trades.
  • Around 20 minutes to treat a bath, so several bedrooms can be done in a single visit.
  • Enamel baths are back in use immediately — no cure time at all. Acrylic shower trays cure overnight, so the room is back in service the next day, not the next week.
  • Whole-estate rollouts. Multiple rooms, or multiple sites, planned as one programme.
  • It still looks like your bathroom. The treatment works on the surface's microscopic texture, so appearance is usually unchanged.
  • Out-of-hours work available, so bedrooms can be done around your bookings.

Questions

Bath and shower tray treatment: your questions.

Do you have to remove the bath or shower tray?

No. The treatment works on the surface itself, so the fitting stays exactly where it is. No plumber, no tiler, no re-tiling.

How long does it take?

Around 20 minutes to treat a bath, so several bedrooms can be done in a single visit. Larger jobs — multiple rooms or multiple sites — are planned as one programme.

When can the bath be used again?

Enamel baths can be used straight away — there is no cure time. Acrylic shower trays cure overnight, so the room is back in service the next day.

Will it change how the bath looks?

Usually not. The treatment works on the surface's microscopic texture, so appearance is usually unchanged.

Can you work around our guests?

Yes. Out-of-hours work is available, so bedrooms can be done around your bookings.

How long does the treatment last?

Our treatments typically last up to 3 years with the right maintenance.

Do you cover my area?

Yes. Rodal works nationwide and has done since 2003. We're based in Banwell, North Somerset.
